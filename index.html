Hulkrunners

Friends-only cooperative space looter-shooter builds.

Latest release: m4-fb6d4f8

  • Download for macOS (137 MB)

This build starts a fresh save with every ship part and the Cutter, Courier and Barge unlocked. Everyone in a crew needs this same build.

Steam must be running and signed in. See the included HOW TO RUN notes.

What's new

m4-fb6d4f8

  • Smooth crew play: ships and enemies no longer jump or rubber-band with four players, and bigger crews meet a few fewer enemies.
  • A new soundtrack of about 20 songs: calm music while you explore, combat music during surges, and Old Rattler's own songs.
  • Middle-click pings for the crew: a "look at this" marker, an enemy or a loot item. The Q menu is gone.
  • Player names under ships and drones; hold Alt to show enemy and loot names.
  • Bigger loot and caches that stay easy to spot zoomed out, and bigger crew icons on the radar.
  • Construction mode crosses out taken colors, marks fitted modules, shows who is ready, and remembers your fit for each ship.
  • Old Rattler fires faster, and his missiles fly faster, track better and explode.
  • Movement: an elastic slingshot Grapple, a punchier Afterburner and Ram charge, and heavy builds never fly slower than their ship's base speed.
  • The Warden Bay's craft have real ship art; the Napalm cloud is weaker; damage numbers are sharp at every zoom.

m4-0269766

  • Bigger crews meet more enemies: a crew of three meets twice a solo pilot's, and enemies get +15% health per extra pilot.
  • Old Rattler gets +75% health per extra pilot, and his battle damage is easier to see when zoomed out.
  • Guests see the damage numbers for their own hits.

m4-e8eb1f9

  • Guests flying in the crew's run no longer get the rejoin-or-abandon panel.

m4-01260d9

  • Retro arcade sound and music, with Music, Menu and Effects volume in Settings.
  • Killstreak: every kill stacks extra speed, shown in a bar above your skills.
  • Extracting takes you straight home, where a loot panel lists what you brought back.
  • A simpler pickup card: F to fit, X to keep.
  • Construction mode outlines only the part you're changing, shows a new core at once, hides parts that don't fit and explains each pairing.
  • Old Rattler rams harder, fires homing missiles from one turret and flashes only where he's hit, in a clearer arena.
  • Tougher enemies; automatic weapons deal less damage than fixed ones, guided ones in between.
  • The Grapple aims itself a little; the mining and transport Barges warp in and show their shields.
  • Thicker, longer engine trails, and bots with funny names.
