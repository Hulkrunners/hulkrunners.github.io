Hulkrunners

Friends-only cooperative space looter-shooter builds.

Latest release: m4-5ad04260

  • Download for macOS (220 MB)
  • Download for Windows (188 MB)

This build starts a fresh save with every ship part and the Cutter, Courier and Barge unlocked. Everyone in a crew needs this same build.

Steam must be running and signed in. See the included HOW TO RUN notes.

What's new

m4-5ad04260

  • A whole new soundtrack: 21 songs for the mothership, calm flight, surges, killstreaks and each of Old Rattler's three phases.
  • Every playlist shuffles, never plays the same song twice in a row, and picks a fresh song when you start the game, come home to the mothership or launch an excursion.
  • Old Rattler's music now changes on the beat when he enters a new phase.

m4-10ad96f9

  • A new combat UI in painted frames: ship status with Shields, Armor, reactor and cargo gauges, the system circles, the radar with a menu button, the sector frame and crew portraits.
  • A comms panel whose red lamp blinks when an event starts or a priority message comes in.
  • The Esc menu, map and ship panel in the same style; the Esc menu has a CREW button for the party list.
  • Text chat is gone; use pings (middle mouse) to talk to the crew.

m4-f4269fb9

  • Elites are generated: each is a bigger gunner, rammer, drone or bruiser with its own mix of one movement, one defensive and one weapon trick, from wardens and missiles to lasers.
  • Some elites are Carriers that launch their own drones.
  • Elites wear a platinum star at each end of their health bar.

m4-eec1c2b5

  • The Fabricator rolls each affix at random; pin the ones you want for 30% extra per slot.
  • More engrams: elites drop an engram 60% of the time, and Old Rattler drops two per player.
  • The transport and mining ship arrive through a warp tunnel, with a much bigger beacon. Every event pings and shows on the radar, and a second transport has to be called at least 1,500 m from the first.
  • Old Rattler no longer heals while anyone is in his arena.
  • A dead pilot can leave the run, and guests no longer freeze when the host quits.
  • E always opens the terminal you're at; answer a ready check by clicking READY.
  • Fixes: the shredder opens quickly, the mouse wheel no longer zooms behind the party list or construction screen, the Refinery no longer draws over your drone, and the window's scale is right from the start.

m4-308af494

  • Crafting is here. Engrams decode on a three-reel slot machine at the Relic Core: pull the lever, and three of a kind upgrades the engram to high-grade.
  • Module blueprints wait on the blueprint rack in Engineering. Craft them at the Fabricator: pick one affix per slot, and spend extra Scrap Iron to push the output to the top.
  • Modules are ranked Mk I to Mk X within their rarity, from a weak roll to a top roll.
  • The reroll bench rerolls one affix on a module, and the shredder turns unwanted modules, blueprints and spare part copies back into Scrap Iron.
  • Rarity outlines now always show on modules in construction mode.
  • Alpha now favors Interceptor, Battleship and Prospector engrams.

m4-55078b94

  • Smoother crew play: a crew of six runs 15–45% lighter, and Old Rattler's last phase with six ships no longer bogs down.
  • Alpha-1 is easier for starter ships (enemies hit and last less); Alpha-2 is the full-strength sector.
  • A dropped player can rejoin the crew's run as long as a Warp Charge is left.

m4-fb6d4f8

  • Smooth crew play: ships and enemies no longer jump or rubber-band with four players, and bigger crews meet a few fewer enemies.
  • A new soundtrack of about 20 songs: calm music while you explore, combat music during surges, and Old Rattler's own songs.
  • Middle-click pings for the crew: a "look at this" marker, an enemy or a loot item. The Q menu is gone.
  • Player names under ships and drones; hold Alt to show enemy and loot names.
  • Bigger loot and caches that stay easy to spot zoomed out, and bigger crew icons on the radar.
  • Construction mode crosses out taken colors, marks fitted modules, shows who is ready, and remembers your fit for each ship.
  • Old Rattler fires faster, and his missiles fly faster, track better and explode.
  • Movement: an elastic slingshot Grapple, a punchier Afterburner and Ram charge, and heavy builds never fly slower than their ship's base speed.
  • The Warden Bay's craft have real ship art; the Napalm cloud is weaker; damage numbers are sharp at every zoom.

m4-0269766

  • Bigger crews meet more enemies: a crew of three meets twice a solo pilot's, and enemies get +15% health per extra pilot.
  • Old Rattler gets +75% health per extra pilot, and his battle damage is easier to see when zoomed out.
  • Guests see the damage numbers for their own hits.

m4-e8eb1f9

  • Guests flying in the crew's run no longer get the rejoin-or-abandon panel.

m4-01260d9

  • Retro arcade sound and music, with Music, Menu and Effects volume in Settings.
  • Killstreak: every kill stacks extra speed, shown in a bar above your skills.
  • Extracting takes you straight home, where a loot panel lists what you brought back.
  • A simpler pickup card: F to fit, X to keep.
  • Construction mode outlines only the part you're changing, shows a new core at once, hides parts that don't fit and explains each pairing.
  • Old Rattler rams harder, fires homing missiles from one turret and flashes only where he's hit, in a clearer arena.
  • Tougher enemies; automatic weapons deal less damage than fixed ones, guided ones in between.
  • The Grapple aims itself a little; the mining and transport Barges warp in and show their shields.
  • Thicker, longer engine trails, and bots with funny names.
