Hulkrunners

Friends-only cooperative space looter-shooter builds.

Latest release: m4-156343ad5

  • Download for macOS (518 MB)
  • Download for Windows (487 MB)

This build starts a fresh save with every ship part and the Cutter, Courier and Barge unlocked. Everyone in a crew needs this same build.

Steam must be running and signed in. See the included HOW TO RUN notes.

What's new

m4-156343ad5

  • Nine tier 2 ships to fly, with underslung, dual top and large weapon mounts.
  • The Gravity Grapple, a new weapon: latch onto a ship, a rock or a crewmate and reel in.
  • The Phase Drive replaces the old Grapple: fly through rocks, wrecks and ships.
  • Upgrade modules now have abilities that fire by themselves, and the injector is now a part, the Nanite Injector.
  • The mothership's rooms are rebuilt piece by piece in the style of its new outside.
  • Blown hulk panels open onto real hold interiors, the ship panel shows a bigger ship, and the Skiff is 10% smaller.
  • Fixes for pings on a bad connection and comet paths that folded back on themselves.

m4-327685efb

  • The director is harder: shorter calms, bigger surges and longer peaks.
  • The construction screen is now your ship as a diagram, with a box beside the hull for each slot. Slots have plain names, and the bottom panel says what a switch does, with the numbers that change. Arrow keys or a pad step through it.
  • The list on the left is now Loadouts, Parts and Modules. Each tier 1 ship has a Starter loadout, and + New saves your own. Modules show what fits the part you chose.
  • The injector is a button of its own on the ability bar, with its key and charges. A ship with none fitted flies the basic injector.
  • The ship status panel reads Shields, Armor and Reactor from 0 to 100, and the Reactor and hold are half-width bars with icons.
  • Blowable hulk panels have a purple Reinforced bar and 300 Armor, and they don't regenerate. Old Rattler's top bar turns Armor yellow.
  • Cryo and Nanite now show in flight and on health bars: Frost grows over a hull in pale ice, and Nanite swarms in neon green.
  • A big mining asteroid now waits until a pilot presses Interact, and flying past starts nothing. Its guards wake when you come within 380 m, hit them or mine it directly.
  • The transport Barge has two tractor turrets with a longer reach. The mining Barge has a second turret and lasers that sweep, and it parks on the side of the rock clear of other things.
  • The game now tells you only where to find boss intel, not where Old Rattler hides.
  • Killing Old Rattler during a distress call now pays. A convoy no one finds warps out after 120 s. The ore refinery's ambushes are scavengers now.
  • The world seed is hidden, and the New survey button is gone.
  • Shorter loading: trips home hold about a second, down from nearly two.
  • Known issues: on a bad connection, some effects or pings occasionally don't show for another player. An event Barge was seen once looking sliced and mirrored. Big mining may be very hard late in a run. A ship can slide off to the right just after the loading screen on a second launch.

m4-380efad9

  • The director is rebuilt. He reads your crew and picks his tactics: cut-offs, hooking whoever carries most, running down a straggler. Watch the radar for shadows before a surge, listen for the scav dispatcher on comms, and follow the bearings on the warning. Surges come more often, and elites come sooner for crews that clear fast. The surge countdown is gone from the HUD.
  • Missions: pick one on the Bridge's star map before you launch, from three offers by grade. It pays a module, an engram or a blueprint for each pilot. It costs a stake from the shared pool, returned if you finish. Each mission has five steps, hidden until they come, shown under the radar. Eight missions so far, including carrying cargo, luring an elite and escorting a tug.
  • One seed now decides each world, so a mission can promise what the run holds.
  • Cryo, a new weapon: Frost builds to Brittle and holds Shields down, then a hit from any other weapon shatters the target and sends shards to its neighbors.
  • Nanite, a new weapon: a swarm eats Armor once Shields are down, jumps to the next enemy when its target dies, and returns a little Armor to you.
  • Every part has a Cryo and a Nanite version, and module families now decode four categories each.
  • Maneuver Reserve, a new crew upgrade: 1,000 Scrap Iron, and every movement system on the crew holds one more charge. Old Field and Engine modules are gone from saves, with no refund.
  • Much faster loading: the first Bridge visit no longer hitches, and launches and trips home hold far shorter stalls.
  • The ore stronghold is back toward its concept, with its pipes and reactor joined. The ore refinery's belts and rails now connect. Art is still being worked on for both.
  • Eleven enemy pieces reworked so their tops catch the light, most visibly Old Rattler's turrets.
  • Known issues: the director may still feel too easy, and more tuning is coming. On a bad connection a ping at an empty spot occasionally doesn't show for the host. Killing the boss during a distress call doesn't pay yet.

m4-8f8ca3a4

  • The Alpha sector is now a solar system: rings, belts, planets and stations round a Sun you can't fly through. Its wind pushes you back and burns. The closer in you go, the richer and more dangerous it gets.
  • The map is rebuilt for the round sector, with a legend of what each icon means.
  • When you die you leave a wreck. A crewmate can start a repair on it: a transport warps in and its drones bring you back.
  • All six megaships are now lit by the Sun as you fly round them, with new art on the ore stronghold and the security carrier, whose engines burn.
  • Blowable doors are rebuilt to fit each hulk: riveted plates with a hazard X, and blown ones leave a torn, scorched rim. Hulk turrets now take each hulk's own colors.
  • New rocks, comet and mining asteroids. The Cutter has new art, with its ball turret further back.
  • The Bridge and Engine Room are redrawn on steel decks to match the Dry Dock, with the Engine Room at the rear.
  • Field and Engine modules are gone. Any you had leave their slots and are paid for once in Scrap Iron.
  • Shots now end on the first thing they hit, and the Bore Lance pierces and leaks through shields.
  • The distress hauler now marks its arrival with a beacon and warps in when a crewmate comes close, or after 90 seconds. It collides on its real outline.
  • Repairs no longer delay surges, and an elite's laser now stops on an objective and hurts it.
  • On a bad connection, a press at a wreck or other target is no longer lost silently: it's held for a moment, and you're told if you need to fly closer. Far-off enemies no longer leave ghosts for guests to shoot at.
  • Known issues: the first visit to the Bridge may hitch for a moment, and on a bad connection a ping at an empty spot occasionally doesn't show for the host.

m4-c638096d

  • Three new derelict megaships join the map's pool: an ore refinery, a security carrier with burnt wrecks on its deck, and a sleek hauler. The cargo carrier now lies broken in two across a debris field.
  • New events: the Scav Convoy (stop the Scav Transports before they warp out), the Shakedown (pay the toll or fight), the Black Box Sweep (reach the data caches in time), and the comet.
  • A new distress call: a shielded hauler warps in with a broken drive. Hold off its stalkers, bring it Scrap Iron, and watch it warp out.
  • Scav Transports have shields and turrets, and come in three kinds.
  • A redesigned event panel with the countdown and the count that matters, and clearer skull markers on elites and the map.
  • New front mounts with twin guns on brackets, cleaner noses, and sleeker hulls without the utility bay's art. Your utility module still works.
  • The Engine Room and the Bridge are rebuilt, with a walkway around the core and a new view out of the Bridge's canopy.
  • Known issue: the first warp tunnel you see may hitch for a moment.

m4-582a5b89

  • The mothership is rebuilt as three rooms in a line: the Engine Room, a brand-new Dry Dock where every ship has its berth, and the Bridge.
  • One Fabricator does it all: crafting, rerolls and salvage, plus the new Slam, which pushes a module higher at the risk of bricking it, and the Power Slam that can reach Mk XI.
  • Craft spare copies of a ship part when none is free.
  • Mothership upgrades bought from the shared Scrap Iron pool: Tow Rigs (a bigger tractor beam), Long Ears (boss intel at the start of each excursion) and Warp Capacitors (more Warp Charges).
  • Every Alpha map has three giant derelict megaships to fly over: blow open their cargo panels, crack vaults, watch for ambushes and turrets, and find the one Old Rattler bursts out of.
  • A new weapon module, the Void: gravity wells that drag a pack together and crush it, pulses that throw enemies away, and anchors that pin them in place.
  • The Scav rammer is now a void bomber that blows up into a field that slows you.
  • The Arc weapon is reworked, with branching, flickering lightning.
  • Save your ship builds in construction mode, name them and load them later.
  • The ready check shows a countdown everyone can see, and if you miss the launch you can still join your crew's run.
  • Warp Charges are set at the launch by everyone aboard: everyone's first entry is free, and each rejoin or late newcomer costs a charge.
  • HUD improvements, and big performance gains across the game.

m4-308af494

  • Module blueprints wait on the blueprint rack in Engineering. Craft them at the Fabricator: pick one affix per slot, and spend extra Scrap Iron to push the output to the top.
  • Modules are ranked Mk I to Mk X within their rarity, from a weak roll to a top roll.
  • The reroll bench rerolls one affix on a module, and the shredder turns unwanted modules, blueprints and spare part copies back into Scrap Iron.
  • Rarity outlines now always show on modules in construction mode.
  • Alpha now favors Interceptor, Battleship and Prospector engrams.
