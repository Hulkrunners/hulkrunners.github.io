Hulkrunners Alpha

Breaker's Reach is a graveyard of ships too big to die quietly. Derelict hulks drift around a dying sun, scavengers pick at their bones, and the Meridian Claims Authority still guards what it says it owns. Not everything out there is as dead as it looks.

You fly out from your mothership, crack the hulks open, take what you can carry, and fight your way home. What you bring back makes your crew stronger. What you lose stays out there.

What waits for you

  • Unlock new ships as you grow, from your starting Skiff to hulking Dreadnoughts.
  • A web of stars to chart your path, and choices that shape your pilot.
  • Five Threat Levels, each deeper and stranger than the last.
  • Notorious names hiding among the wrecks, waiting for the wrong crew to come looking.
  • A crew that matters: fly together, fight together, and bring each other home.

This build

The first Alpha. The Reach runs deeper, the ships fly further, and whatever's out there has noticed you.
